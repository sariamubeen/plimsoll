A bar says “not available on this site”

That is Plimsoll being honest rather than broken. Anthropic publishes a usage page, so Claude’s session, weekly and credit meters are real readings. OpenAI and Google publish no equivalent, so those figures cannot be read at all. Plimsoll says so instead of showing an empty bar, which would read as “0% used”.

Why is the context number an estimate?

It is counted from the text on the page, and it is a lower bound. Long conversations are virtualised, so older messages are not in the page at all, and system instructions are never visible. Treat the trend as more meaningful than the absolute figure. You can tune the characters-per-token constant and the context ceiling in Options.
